Privacy Policy

At Everyday Strength, I respect your privacy and am committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website, contact me, or schedule a consultation, and how that information is used.

Information I Collect

When you contact me or schedule a consultation, I may receive information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, and any details you choose to share.

The website may also involve the automatic collection of basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser information, by the services used to display website content.

How Your Information Is Used

I use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, communicate about coaching services, and provide services you request.

I do not sell your personal information or use it to send unsolicited marketing communications.

Appointment Scheduling

Consultations are scheduled through Calendly. When you book an appointment, Calendly collects information needed to arrange your consultation, such as your name and email address.

For more information, please review Calendly's Privacy Notice.

Website Fonts and Images

This website uses Google Fonts and images hosted by Unsplash. When your browser loads these resources, it connects to their servers, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.

You can learn more about their practices by reviewing the Google Fonts privacy information and Unsplash Privacy Policy.

Information Sharing

I do not share or sell your personal information for third-party marketing purposes. Information may be processed by the services identified above as necessary to operate the website or arrange consultations.

Data Security and Retention

I take reasonable precautions to protect the personal information you provide. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Your Privacy Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information I hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, please contact alcohanim@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the website, its services, or applicable privacy requirements. Updates will be posted on this page.
